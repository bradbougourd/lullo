Lullo privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Lullo is a baby tracker for families. It is run by Brad Bougourd in Jersey, who is responsible for your data (the "data controller"). This page explains what Lullo keeps, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

What Lullo stores

  • Your first name, and the account Apple gives us when you use Sign in with Apple. That includes an email address, which Apple can hide behind a relay address. If you sign in with email instead, we keep that email.
  • Your baby's first name, date of birth, and sex if you add it (only used to draw the right growth chart).
  • What you log: sleeps, feeds, nappies, pumping, solids and allergens, medicines and doses, temperatures, symptoms, illnesses, appointments, vaccinations, allergies, weight and height, unsettled spells, notes and milestones.
  • Month by month photos, if you add them.
  • Which family member logged each entry, so your partner can see who did what.
  • For sitter links: the link's name, the plan and notes you choose to share, and the name the sitter types in.
  • When someone tries a wrong family code, a short record so repeated guessing can be blocked.

Why, and on what basis

Lullo keeps this to run the app for your family: to sync between your phones, predict naps and show your history. Some of what you log is health information about your child, which the law treats as special. Lullo only stores it with your explicit consent, which you give when you set up or join a family. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (below).

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase in their London data centre. Only signed-in members of your family can read or change it. It's encrypted while it travels and while it's stored, but it isn't end-to-end encrypted, so the person who runs Lullo could technically access it. We would only look at it to fix a problem you've asked us to look at.

A copy is kept on your phone so the app and lock screen buttons work offline, plus a daily backup for the last 14 days in the Files app. Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Signing out removes all of these from the phone.

Lock screen buttons log sleeps, feeds and nappies without unlocking, and need two taps so nothing logs by accident. Undo needs Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Anyone holding your phone can see the lock screen card, which shows your baby's name and current sleep or feed.

Who sees it

Only the people in your family on Lullo, and anyone you choose to share with: a handover summary, a GP summary, an export, or a grandparent or sitter link.

A sitter link shows your baby's first name, sleeps, feeds and nappies from the last 36 hours, which medicine was given and when (so nobody doubles a dose), and any notes you add. It doesn't show health details like temperatures, symptoms or growth. It lets them log sleeps, bottles and nappies until it expires (31 days at most) or you turn it off. Only send it to people you trust.

Family codes expire after 7 days. The person who set up the family can remove anyone, which also makes a new code and turns off sitter links.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else. We don't use tracking or analytics tools.

Other companies involved

  • Supabase stores the data (London).
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and delivers the app through TestFlight.
  • GitHub hosts this page and the sitter page, so it sees the internet address of anyone who opens them. The sitter link's code is kept after a # in the address, so it isn't sent to GitHub.

How long it's kept

  • Your family's logs and photos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Sitter links: deleted 7 days after they expire.
  • Wrong family code records: deleted after a day.
  • Backups on your phone: the last 14 days, removed when you sign out.
  • Supabase may keep its own encrypted backups for a short time after something is deleted.

Your rights

You can see and export everything in the app (Family, then Export and backup), correct any entry, and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, using the contact below. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner (JOIC) or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Health information

Lullo's nap times and growing-up guide are general guidance, not medical advice. If you're worried about your baby, speak to your health visitor, GP or call 111 (999 in an emergency).

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Family, then Delete my account and data. This removes you straight away, along with any sitter links you made. If nobody else is left in your family, your family, babies, every log, photos and sitter links are deleted for good, and the copy on your phone is removed.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: email bradbougourd@gmail.com, or use the feedback button in TestFlight.
